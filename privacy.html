Privacy Policy

Balaji NextGen Solutions · Siliguri, West Bengal, India · Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Who we are

Balaji NextGen Solutions ("we", "us") provides the Balaji NextGen ERP, Growth Hub, QR menu, table ordering and WhatsApp messaging tools used by restaurants and businesses ("our clients"). This policy explains what data we handle when guests and staff use these tools, including through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta).

2. Data we collect

  • Guest details given to a restaurant: name, mobile / WhatsApp number, visit and order history, bills, feedback ratings, reservations and event tickets.
  • WhatsApp messages a guest sends to a restaurant's WhatsApp number (for example feedback replies, check-in, STOP), and delivery / read status of messages the restaurant sends.
  • Consent choices, such as whether a guest agreed to receive offers.
  • Staff and business data entered into the ERP by our clients (users, roles, sales, stock, accounts).

3. How we use it

  • To run the restaurant's services: orders, bills, receipts, reservations, tickets and feedback.
  • To send WhatsApp messages on behalf of the restaurant: bill and order updates, reminders, and offers only to guests who opted in.
  • To show the restaurant reports about its own business.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use one restaurant's guest data for any other restaurant.

4. Sharing

  • The restaurant you visited, which owns its guest data.
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform), to deliver WhatsApp messages.
  • Google (Google Workspace / Apps Script) and Cloudflare, which host and relay the data securely.
  • Authorities, only when required by law.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored in the restaurant's own secured Google storage, accessible only to that restaurant's authorised staff and to us for support. Access keys are kept on the server and are never shown in web pages. Data is kept while the restaurant uses our service, or until deletion is requested.

6. Stop WhatsApp messages

Reply STOP to the restaurant's WhatsApp number at any time. You will no longer receive offers. Messages about your own bills or orders may still be sent if you place an order.

7. Data deletion instructions

To delete your data:

  • Ask the restaurant you visited to delete your guest record, or
  • Send DELETE MY DATA from your mobile number to the restaurant's WhatsApp number, or
  • Contact Balaji NextGen Solutions through www.balajinextgen.in, giving your mobile number and the restaurant's name.

We remove your name, number, messages and feedback within 30 days. Bills already issued may be kept only where tax law (GST) requires it.

8. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, and withdraw consent for offers, as provided under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
