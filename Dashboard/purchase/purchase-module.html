🧾 GST Input Tax Credit (ITC) Treatment
Standalone restaurants under the 5% GST rate (not the 18% AC-restaurant rate) generally cannot claim ITC on purchases — the GST paid becomes a real, non-recoverable cost. Most other businesses can claim ITC — GST paid is a recoverable credit, not a cost. This changes real numbers: when ITC is reversed, GST gets added into product cost and Stock Value; when ITC is claimed, GST is tracked separately as Input CGST/SGST/IGST and stock cost excludes it.
