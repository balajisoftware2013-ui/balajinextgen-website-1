🟢 Credit Note (from Supplier): Supplier issues this to reduce the amount you owe. Used for price corrections, discounts after invoice, short supply adjustments, GST corrections. No stock movement. Accounting: Dr Sundry Creditor → Cr Purchase A/c (or Discount Received).

Debit Note

🔴 Debit Note — to Supplier
🔴 Debit Note (issued by us to supplier): We raise this when we return goods or claim a reduction. This is the mirror document to Purchase Return — sent to supplier to formally claim credit. Used for purchase returns, short delivery claims, damage claims. Accounting: Dr Sundry Creditor → Cr Purchase Return / Claim A/c.
