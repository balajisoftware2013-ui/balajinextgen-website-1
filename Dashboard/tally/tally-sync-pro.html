Quick help: what do these words mean?
  • Ledger: an account name in Tally, like HDFC Bank or Rent.
  • Post: send the entries into Tally. Nothing reaches Tally before you press Post to Tally.
  • Map / Match: only a preview. The app picks a ledger for every entry and shows the list so you can check it.
  • Rule: a word in the bank description that decides the ledger (example: CHARGES goes to Bank Charges).
  • Suspense: entries the app could not match. They are held, nothing is lost. Pick the ledger once and the app remembers it.
  • Yellow rows (Google Sheet): already sent to Tally, so they are skipped next time. No double entries.
  • Download XML: a file you can import into Tally yourself, instead of posting directly.
  • Advanced: extra sections for experts. You can leave them off.

Not verified. Posting is not tested against a live Tally in this build; use "Download XML" as a fallback. Shortcuts: Ctrl+Enter post, Alt+M map ledgers.

Tally not connecting? Quick fix
  1. In Tally press F1 (Help), Settings, Connectivity.
  2. Set Tally to act as Server, port 9000, then restart Tally.
  3. Open your company in Tally and keep it open.
  4. On a phone? Tally runs on your PC. Put the PC's address in "Tally address", like http://192.168.1.10:9000, with phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi. If the page is opened from https, start the connector on the PC instead.
  5. Press "Tally connected" at the top of this page to check again.
  6. Still blocked by the browser? Start the Tally connector (Start_Tally_Connector.bat) and check again.
