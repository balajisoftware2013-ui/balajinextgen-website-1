Help & guide

Answers to common questions

Tally does not connect
  • Tally must be open with the company loaded, and TallyPrime's XML port enabled (F1 Help › Settings › Connectivity › Client/Server: Both, port 9000).
  • Run START_TALLY_CONNECTOR on the same PC and keep its window open — it listens on 127.0.0.1:9001.
  • Type the company name exactly as Tally shows it, then press Test connection on the Dashboard.
Some vouchers say "need review"

They were not imported. Usual reasons: a ledger the tool could not recognise (rename tax ledgers with CGST / SGST / IGST / Cess / Round off), TDS / TCS lines, IGST and CGST in the same voucher, or a duplicate invoice number. Fix in Tally and capture again.

Which files can I upload?
  • GSTR-2B JSON — gst.gov.in › Returns › GSTR-2B › Download JSON.
  • Filed GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B — PDF or JSON. Or open the PDF, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C and use 📋 Paste.
  • Purchase / sales register CSV (columns: Invoice Number, Invoice Date, GSTIN, taxable, cgst, sgst, igst, cess, total).
Automatic: folder, auto-save, auto Tally
  • Folder (Chrome / Edge): Dashboard › Choose GST folder — pick one folder per client, e.g. Downloads\GST\VANNS. Save the downloaded GSTR-2B JSON and the filed GSTR-1 / 3B (PDF or JSON) there; new files are read every 2 minutes while this page is open and the reconciliation updates by itself. After restarting the browser, click Reconnect folder once.
  • Sales / purchase CSV files are read too when the file name contains “sales” or “purchase”.
  • Exports (GSTR-1 JSON, CSVs) are saved in Balaji_GST_Exports inside the folder — click Open next to any file to view it.
  • Drop files anywhere on the page to read them at once.
  • Auto-save keeps everything on this device; the last client opens by itself next time.
  • Auto Tally: tick “Auto every … min” in the Tally card — it is remembered, and the current month is captured each time the page opens.
Does it file my return?

No. It prepares and checks: GSTR-1 JSON for the GST Offline Tool, a GSTR-3B draft, GSTR-9 / 9C workpapers. You review and file on gst.gov.in. Direct portal fetch needs a GSP connection and taxpayer OTP consent.

What does "Probable match" mean?

The supplier wrote the invoice number differently (INV/012/25-26 vs 12) or the date differs, but the GSTIN and tax agree. Review once and choose Accept.

What is the Sec 16(4) date?

ITC of a financial year can be claimed only up to 30 November of the next year (or the annual return date if earlier). Pending ITC shows the days left for every invoice not yet in 2B.

Where is my data? How do I move to another PC?

On this device, separately for each client ID + GSTIN. Press Save on this device after work. To move or back up, use Export workpaper and on the other PC Import JSON.

Glossary

GSTR-1 — your sales return, due 11th of next month.
GSTR-3B — monthly summary and tax payment, due 20th of next month.
GSTR-2B — ITC statement built from your suppliers' GSTR-1.
ITC — GST paid on purchases, set off against tax on sales.
B2B / B2CS / B2CL — registered buyers / small consumer sales / inter-state consumer invoices above ₹1 lakh.
RCM — reverse charge: the buyer pays the tax.
IMS — Invoice Management System: accept / reject / pending on supplier invoices.
GSTR-9 / 9C — annual return and reconciliation statement.
