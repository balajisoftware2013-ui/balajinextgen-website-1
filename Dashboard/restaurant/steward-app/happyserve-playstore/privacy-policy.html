Privacy Policy — HAPPYSERVE Steward

Operated by Balaji NextGen Solutions, Siliguri, West Bengal, India · Last updated: 4 October 2026

HAPPYSERVE Steward ("the App") is a restaurant staff app used by employees of restaurants that subscribe to Balaji NextGen ERP. It is not intended for the general public or for children.

Data we process

Account data: user code, name, role and login credentials issued by the restaurant. Business data: tables, orders, KOTs, bills, guest count, and, where the restaurant enables it, customer name and mobile number entered by staff. Location: only when a staff member uses a feature that records GPS location (for example attendance or delivery location), and only after the device asks for permission. Device data: app settings and an offline order queue stored on the device so orders are not lost without internet.

How data is used

Only to run the restaurant's operations: taking orders, sending KOTs, billing, reports and staff performance/incentive calculation for the restaurant that employs the user. We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

Where data is stored

Data is stored in the restaurant's own Balaji NextGen ERP database (hosted on Google Cloud / Google Workspace services) and transmitted over HTTPS.

Sharing

Data is visible only to authorised users of the same restaurant and to Balaji NextGen Solutions for support. It is not shared with third parties except where required by law.

Permissions

Location (optional, feature-specific) and notifications (optional, for order alerts). Both can be turned off in device settings.

Retention and deletion

Business records are kept as long as the restaurant's subscription requires them for accounting and tax purposes. To delete a staff account or request data deletion, contact the restaurant administrator or email us below.
