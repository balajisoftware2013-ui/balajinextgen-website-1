## 💡 Three Ways to Import Invoices

### Method 1: Excel Bulk Import (Fastest for Multiple)
```
Perfect for: Multiple invoices from emails/documents
Time: 2-3 minutes for 10 invoices
Steps:
  1. Download template
  2. Fill Excel
  3. Upload
  4. Import all at once
```

**Example:**
```
Collected 20 invoices during the week
→ Fill Excel with all 20
→ Upload in bulk
→ All saved in 2 minutes
→ Total time saved: 3+ hours
```

### Method 2: AI Invoice Scan (Fastest for Single)
```
Perfect for: Single invoice processing
Time: 1-2 minutes per invoice
Steps:
  1. Take photo or have PDF
  2. Upload
  3. AI reads automatically
  4. Review & save
```

**Example:**
```
Vendor brings invoice
→ Take photo
→ Upload to system
→ AI extracts: amount, items, date, GST
→ Review (30 seconds)
→ Save
→ Done in 2 minutes
```

### Method 3: Manual Entry (Traditional, Always Works)
```
Perfect for: Edge cases, complex invoices
Time: 10-15 minutes per invoice
Steps:
  1. Open Purchase form
  2. Fill all fields
  3. Save
```
