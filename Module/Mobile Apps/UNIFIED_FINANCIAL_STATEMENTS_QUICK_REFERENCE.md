# 📊 UNIFIED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS — Quick Reference

## One Click → Three Reports

```
Click Financial Reports (or Press Alt+F)
    ↓
Opens view with THREE TABS:
    ├─ 📊 Balance Sheet (default)
    ├─ 📈 P&L Statement
    └─ 💵 Cash Flow

Click tab to switch between them!
```

---

## 🎯 What Each Tab Shows

### Balance Sheet
```
ASSETS                  LIABILITIES + CAPITAL
├─ Cash      ₹50K      ├─ Supplier Due: ₹120K

├─ Receivables ₹85K
```

### P&L Statement
```
Sales              ₹500,000
- COGS            -₹250,000
= Gross Profit     ₹250,000 (50%)
- Expenses        -₹80,000
= Net Profit       ₹170,000 (34% margin)
```

### Cash Flow
```
Operating Cash:
├─ Cash Sales         +₹150K
├─ Cash Purchases     -₹120K
├─ Cash Expenses      -₹30K
└─ Net               ₹0

Cash Position:
├─ Cash in Hand       ₹50K
├─ Bank              ₹200K
└─ Total Liquid      ₹250K (45 days runway)
```

## ✅ Healthy Signs

| Tab | Healthy | Unhealthy |
|-----|---------|-----------|
| **BS** | ✅ Balanced, Assets growing | ❌ Not balanced, Declining capital |
| **P&L** | ✅ Profitable, Growing margin | ❌ Loss, Low margin, High costs |
| **CF** | ✅ Positive flow, 30+ days | ❌ Negative flow, Low cash, <10 days |

---

## 🎯 Key Numbers to Check

```
Balance Sheet:
  Total Assets: Should be growing
  Capital: Should be positive
  Status: Should be ✅ BALANCED

P&L:
  Net Profit: Should be positive
  Margin %: Should be 20%+
  Growth: Should be increasing

Cash Flow:
  Liquid Cash: Should be >0
  Runway: Should be 30+ days
  Trend: Should be growing
```
