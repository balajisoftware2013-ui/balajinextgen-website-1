## 📊 Each Tab Explained

### Tab 1: Balance Sheet

```
Shows your financial position at a point in time

WHAT IT TELLS YOU:
├─ What you own (Assets)
│  ├─ Cash: Ready to spend
│  ├─ Bank: Saved money
│  ├─ Receivables: Money owed by customers
│  └─ Inventory: Stock value
│
├─ What you owe (Liabilities)
│  └─ Supplier Dues: Money owed to suppliers
│
├─ Your equity (Capital)
│  └─ Owner's Capital: Your net worth
│
└─ Verification
   └─ Assets should equal Liabilities + Capital

HEALTHY INDICATORS:
✅ Balanced (Assets = Liab + Capital)
✅ Positive Owner's Capital
✅ Growing Assets
✅ Controlled Liabilities
```

### Tab 2: P&L Statement

```
Shows how much profit/loss you made in a period

WHAT IT TELLS YOU:
├─ Sales: Total revenue
├─ COGS: Cost of goods sold
├─ Gross Profit: Sales - COGS
├─ Expenses: Operating costs
└─ Net Profit: Bottom line

HEALTHY INDICATORS:
✅ Positive Net Profit
✅ Rising Revenue
✅ Controlled Expenses
✅ Good Profit Margin (20-40%+)

PROFIT MARGIN:
= Net Profit ÷ Sales × 100
= If you sell ₹100, you make (profit margin)%
Example: 34% margin = ₹34 profit per ₹100 sales
```

### Tab 3: Cash Flow Statement

```
Shows your actual cash movements

WHAT IT TELLS YOU:
├─ Cash from Operations
│  ├─ Cash sales
│  ├─ Cash purchases
│  └─ Cash expenses
│
├─ Current Cash Position
│  ├─ Cash in hand
│  ├─ Bank balance
│  └─ Total liquid cash
│
└─ Cash Health Metrics
   ├─ Daily burn rate
   ├─ Days of runway
   └─ Survival time

HEALTHY INDICATORS:
✅ Positive Cash Flow
✅ Growing Cash Balance
✅ 30+ days of runway
✅ Positive cash from operations
```

## ✅ Healthy Business Checklist

### Balance Sheet Health
- [ ] Total Assets > 0
- [ ] Capital > 0 (positive equity)
- [ ] Assets = Liabilities + Capital (balanced)
- [ ] Assets growing month-over-month
- [ ] Controlled liabilities

### P&L Health
- [ ] Net Profit > 0 (profitable)
- [ ] Profit Margin > 20% (healthy)
- [ ] Sales growing month-over-month
- [ ] Expenses under control
- [ ] COGS < 50% of sales (good ratio)

### Cash Flow Health
- [ ] Positive cash flow from operations
- [ ] Liquid cash > 30 days expenses
- [ ] Cash not declining
- [ ] Good days of runway (30+ days)
- [ ] No negative cash trends

## 🚨 Warning Signs

### From Balance Sheet
```
❌ Not Balanced
   → Data error, investigate

❌ Negative Capital
   → Liabilities exceed assets, crisis

❌ Declining Assets
   → Losing value, problem

❌ Rising Liabilities
   → Taking more debt, risky
```

### From P&L
```
❌ Negative Profit
   → Losing money

❌ Low Margin < 10%
   → Underpriced, cost problem

❌ Rising Expenses
   → Cost creep, control needed

❌ Declining Sales
   → Market or execution issue
```

### From Cash Flow
```
❌ Negative Cash Flow
   → Burning cash, urgent

❌ Low Liquid Cash
   → Immediate problem

❌ Low Runway < 10 days
   → Crisis, needs immediate action

❌ Cash declining fast
   → Urgently need revenue or cut costs
```
